## Guo Tao

**Guo Tao** is a veteran technology practitioner and author. He works across computational mathematics, artificial intelligence, modern software engineering and data-driven agriculture, with a focus on turning ideas into working systems and turning practice into writing. He has published several books, including *Deep Reinforcement Learning Illustrated*, *Machine Learning Illustrated* and *AI Explainability with Python*.

## Li Yongyong

**Li Yongyong** holds a PhD in Cartography and Geographic Information Systems. He has long taught Python programming, object-oriented programming and GIS applications at university level. His research focuses on applying multi-source remote sensing and machine learning in resource and environmental fields, and in recent years he has focused on the engineering practice of agent technology in teaching and research.
